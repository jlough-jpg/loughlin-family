HISTORY IS A WORK IN PROGRESS

What we know.
What comes next.

Family recollections and memorials give us a place to begin. When sources disagree, we keep the question visible.

When did John Kane and Ellen marry?

Their memorial biographies give July 24, 1849, while the spouse fields give 1847. Their eldest child Dennis is listed as born in August 1847. A marriage register may resolve the difference.

View John’s memorial ↗
Can we document Agnes Quinn’s branch?

The chart names Agnes as William’s wife, with children Helen and Robert W. William’s memorial does not link them. A marriage record and his 1950 obituary are useful next sources.

Who were Thomas and Bridget’s children?

Their memorial biographies name William, John Kane, Elizabeth, and two daughters named Mary, one who died in infancy. Harry, Dennis, and Robert at the chart’s left edge remain unplaced. Old St. Joseph’s baptism registers may help.

Why are there different names for Helen McArdle?

Helen’s own memorial uses Helen Mary; her mother Katherine’s biography uses Helen Denise. A birth or marriage record is needed to reconcile those names.
